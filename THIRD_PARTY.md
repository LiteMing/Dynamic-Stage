## Dependencies and optional integrations

Dynamic Stage is built for Minecraft and uses Architectury, Forge or Fabric,
and SpongePowered Mixin. It can optionally integrate with Distant Horizons,
Voxy, Sinytra Connector, Forgified Fabric API, Embeddium or Sodium, Oculus or
Iris, CMDCam, and CreativeCore. These projects remain subject to their own
licenses and terms. Dynamic Stage's release JARs do not bundle the optional LOD
renderers, camera mods, shader mods, or Connector development runtime.

In particular, Voxy is a separate project whose upstream source currently
states that redistribution is not permitted. The Dynamic Stage MIT License
does not grant permission to redistribute Voxy, a modified Voxy build, or any
other third-party software.

Compatibility code which names or calls a third-party API does not relicense
that third-party project. Obtain each dependency from its authorized source
and follow its license when assembling or distributing a modpack.
